Why Work at WallEye Fund

WallEye Fund offers a unique opportunity to work at the intersection of finance and technology in one of the most dynamic and rapidly evolving industries. We're building something special, and we're looking for exceptional people to join us.

At WallEye, you'll work alongside some of the brightest minds in crypto and finance. You'll have the opportunity to make meaningful contributions, take on significant responsibility, and grow your career in an environment that values innovation, collaboration, and excellence.

What We Offer

  • Competitive compensation packages with performance-based incentives
  • Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance
  • 401(k) retirement plan with company matching
  • Flexible work arrangements and remote work options
  • Professional development opportunities and continuing education support
  • State-of-the-art technology and tools
  • Collaborative and intellectually stimulating work environment
  • Opportunities for career growth and advancement

New York, NY / Remote

Portfolio Manager - Fundamental Crypto
