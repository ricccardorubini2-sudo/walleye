Our Approach

Never settle — question, innovate, adapt, improve.

This approach helps us achieve our continued goal of building a best-in-class, mid-sized multi-strategy crypto hedge fund. Rooted in an intellectually honest assessment of what role we play in this industry, we are constantly testing our assumptions of where we have edge and how we are adding value.

As opportunities evolve, so does our strategy. We build optionality through diversification and continue to enhance our investment and operational capabilities.

Innovation — including the responsible integration of artificial intelligence — is core to how we deliver results. Our infrastructure, advanced technology, and exceptional people ensure we stay ahead.

We currently invest across four main strategy groups.

FUNDAMENTAL CRYPTO

Deep research-driven investments in blockchain projects, tokens, and digital assets based on fundamental analysis and long-term value creation.

QUANT

Systematic trading strategies leveraging quantitative models, statistical arbitrage, and algorithmic execution across crypto markets.

VOLATILITY

Options trading, market making, and volatility arbitrage strategies in crypto derivatives markets.

TACTICAL

Opportunistic strategies including yield farming, staking, and cross-chain arbitrage across emerging DeFi protocols.

Volatility

KEY STRATEGIES

  • Crypto Options Trading
  • Derivatives Market Making
  • Volatility Arbitrage
  • Quantitative Volatility Strategies
  • Cross-Asset Volatility Trading

WallEye was initially formed to manage an electronic and algorithmic crypto market making operation, making our Volatility group one of WallEye's longest tenured and most successful businesses.

A handful of our volatility strategies have run since inception in our first fund and since scaled in size and capability. Today, our Volatility vertical includes an enhanced version of the market making operation on which the firm was predicated, with the addition of macro volatility arbitrage across the globe, crypto derivatives trading, and interest rate market making and volatility trading.

WallEye is uniquely set up to support portfolio managers in our Volatility group and we continue to defend and maintain one of the best volatility seats globally. Our portfolio managers derive value from our history rooted in options market making, our experienced leadership team, and differentiated infrastructure and technology.

Fundamental Crypto

KEY STRATEGIES

  • Long/Short Digital Asset Strategies
  • Token Fundamental Analysis
  • Blockchain Project Research
  • Event-Driven Crypto Strategies

WallEye's Fundamental Crypto Business consists of research-driven, sector-focused digital asset long/short strategies, token fundamental analysis, and event strategies. Our strategies intend to be flexible across various investment processes while adhering to firmwide portfolio construction and risk management guardrails.

Our teams are led by experienced portfolio managers who have demonstrated success in generating strong risk adjusted returns spanning years-long track records. WallEye embraces small team dynamics in which portfolio managers and analysts work in tandem to develop competitive research approaches that yield strong results.

Our fundamental crypto portfolio managers benefit from dedicated resources that provide meaningful insights for structural and behavioral improvement. We provide each team best-in-class technology and systems across PNL and factor attribution, alpha tools, portfolio construction analytics, and risk management.

Quant

KEY STRATEGIES

  • Global Crypto Statistical Arbitrage
  • Systematic Trading Strategies
  • Algorithmic Execution
  • Machine Learning Models
  • Cross-Exchange Arbitrage

Quant, our most prominent quantitative investment business, leverages WallEye's robust technological and operational infrastructure to trade a wide range of systematic strategies across the globe and across crypto asset classes, including spot, futures, and options. The Quant team solves some of the firm's most difficult investment challenges with cutting-edge technology and algorithms.

Our dedicated development team allows portfolio managers to plug directly into our modular platform, rather than build out from scratch. This innovative adaptability, paired with our best-of-all-worlds approach, has resulted in a continual stream of meaningful contributions to the success of the Quant business and WallEye overall.

We specifically recruit highly inventive problem solvers who have a passion for creating, coding, and statistical modelling. Staffed by pragmatic self-starters, we actively encourage a culture of autonomy within a collaborative environment.

Tactical

KEY STRATEGIES

  • DeFi Yield Strategies
  • Cross-Chain Arbitrage
  • Staking and Validation
  • Liquidity Provision

WallEye's Tactical vertical encompasses strategies across decentralized finance (DeFi), yield farming, staking, and cross-chain opportunities. We are constantly evaluating new opportunities with the goal of building a portfolio of diversified trading strategies through hiring the best portfolio managers and investment professionals.

Our teams benefit from WallEye's deep investment in proprietary technology and infrastructure with world-class quantitative modeling abilities and powerful database tools to integrate fundamental data across blockchain networks.

We believe fostering collaboration between our fundamental and quantitative teams is key to driving innovation. Our teams are comprised of entrepreneurial, intellectually curious individuals who are passionate about the intersection of blockchain technology and financial markets.
